Business Rules & Partner Model

Separate product purchase rewards from actual investment records.

Product Purchase Partner

Orders, seller invoices, BV eligibility, vouchers, refunds and reward reversals. Rewards must be tied to qualifying genuine product transactions under published terms.

Actual Investment Partner

Keep this route disabled until counsel approves the legal instrument, eligibility, disclosures, payment collection, filings and accounting. Do not label product points as equity or promise a return.

Changing an indicative rate does not itself create a cash entitlement. Transfer/cash redemption should not be enabled in production until legal, tax, payments and accounting requirements are confirmed.
