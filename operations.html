Before production launch

  • Keep product purchase, company investment and reward-point ledgers legally and financially separate.
  • Do not enable cash redemption or accept investment funds until Indian legal/regulatory and tax review is complete.
  • Use server-side authentication, role-based access and maker-checker approvals for real operations.
  • Use a double-entry ledger, idempotency keys, atomic transfers, immutable audit logs and reconciliation.
  • Publish membership, voucher, refund, privacy, grievance and point-value terms in clear language.
  • Do not describe points as guaranteed returns, deposits or shares unless the legal instrument actually provides those rights.
